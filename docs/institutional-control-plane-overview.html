The Digital-Asset Control Plane for Regulated Financial Institutions

Institution-owned authority. Controlled execution. Traceable evidence.

What institutions retain

The institution owns its customer relationship, financial product, risk policy, approvals, and authoritative financial records. Aetherum supplies governance and orchestration software alongside existing cores. Providers and financial networks perform execution under their contracted responsibilities.

One platform, modular applications

  • Core Governance & Evidence: institution-scoped identity, policy evaluation, pre-execution authorization, and linked decision records.
  • Stablecoin Payments: approved money movement, transaction status, exceptions, and reconciliation across supported execution paths.
  • DACS Lending: digital-asset collateral risk assessment, LTV controls, and supervised lending workflows. The institution remains the lender.
  • Tokenized assets: institution-authorized lifecycle governance and evidence, subject to scope and readiness.

Deployment and diligence

Capability availability differs by integration, environment, and institution. Demonstrations, testnet activity, sandbox access, and partner discussions do not establish production approval. Each engagement defines permitted activity, responsibility allocation, provider dependencies, and production acceptance.

A scoped pilot evaluates approved and denied paths, safe execution, confirmation and reconciliation, evidence retrieval, onboarding effort, operating cost, and recurring commercial value.

Security and operating documentation

This is an architecture and diligence overview, not a security certification or an approved operational runbook. Current deployment-specific signing, key management, access control, incident response, recovery, and provider responsibilities must be validated through institutional diligence.
