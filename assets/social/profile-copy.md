## Company About
Aetherum.ai provides the digital-asset control plane for regulated financial institutions. Our software connects institution-owned identity, policies, and approvals to supported execution providers and financial networks, preserving control and audit evidence across payments, settlement, lending, and tokenized-asset workflows.

Core Governance & Evidence is the common platform. Stablecoin Payments and DACS Lending are modular applications. Institutions retain their financial products, customer relationships, risk policies, and approval authority. Aetherum integrates alongside existing cores.

Availability varies by integration and deployment stage. We define each institutional engagement around a funded use case, clear responsibilities, and measurable operating value.
